About me

Hi, I'm Stefano Adami.

"Scatti a Spasso" comes from the desire to gather the moments when I took the time to stop and look at what so often slips by too fast. To share them the "old-fashioned way". Without social media, algorithms, or likes at any cost.

For me, photography is passion. It's walking quietly between nature and art, mountains and cities. It's standing still waiting for a shift in light or bending down over a tiny detail. I'm not looking for the "perfect" shot.

Between landscapes, wildlife, and macro I try to bring home honest images tied to the moment and place where they were born, without too many frills. If a photo makes you want to lace up your shoes and step outside, it has already done its job.

Photos can be freely downloaded using the dedicated button and republished as long as the source is credited. Zero cookies, zero tracking, just photography.

Interested in a high-resolution photo? Feel free to write to me.

My gear

Canon EOS 5D Mark III camera body
My full-frame pro body when I want to play it safe with zero compromises. With a lens attached it carries noticeable weight, so it comes along on more "intentional" outings rather than casual walks, as well as sports and theatre events.
Canon EOS 50D camera body
More compact and lighter than its pro sibling, it was my first digital camera. Now used only paired with the 5D—when the moment matters more than the weight of two bodies.
Sony RX10 IV camera
1" CMOS sensor, 24–600mm optical zoom (f/2.4–4.0) My most used camera. Light and versatile all-rounder. Lets me go from wide landscapes up to 600mm while keeping quality that has little to envy from a professional setup.
Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra
My safety net against fleeting moments when I don't have a camera with me. Even with a 200 MP main camera, 50 MP ultra-wide, 50 MP 5x telephoto, and 10 MP 3x telephoto, like any smartphone it doesn't compete with dedicated cameras, yet it's enough not to regret leaving them behind.
Canon EF 24-70mm f/2.8L USM lens
Professional standard zoom, f/2.8, ultrasonic The quality workhorse between wide angle and medium telephoto. Bright, solid, and immediate. Easily my most used lens overall.
Canon EF 70-200mm f/2.8L IS USM
Professional telephoto zoom, constant f/2.8, image stabilized Heavy and you feel it. Brought along on planned missions! When I'm out tracking wildlife and distance matters. Compresses perspective and separates the subject with soft bokeh.
Canon EF 100mm f/2.8L Macro IS USM
Macro and medium telephoto lens, f/2.8, image stabilized When I want to dive into tiny details, as well as portraits and close-up views with clean, accurate rendering.
