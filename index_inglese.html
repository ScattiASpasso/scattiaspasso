About me

Hi, I'm Stefano Adami.

"Scatti a Spasso" comes from the desire to gather the moments when I took the time to stop and look at what often passes us by too quickly. To share them the "old-fashioned" way. Without social media, algorithms, and chasing likes at all costs.

For me, photography is a passion. It's walking in silence between nature and art, mountains and cities. It's standing still waiting for a shift in light or bending down over a tiny detail. I don't look for the perfect shot.

Between landscapes, wildlife, and macro, I try to bring home honest images tied to the moment and place where they were born, without too many frills. If a photo makes you want to lace up your shoes and head outside, it has already done its job.

Photos can be freely downloaded using the dedicated button and republished as long as the source is credited. Zero cookies, zero tracking, just photography.

Interested in a high-resolution photo? Feel free to write to me.

My gear

Canon EOS 5D Mark III camera body
My professional full-frame camera for going in with zero compromises. With a lens attached it carries considerable weight, so it comes along on more "important" and never-casual outings, as well as sports and theater events.
Canon EOS 50D camera body
A more compact and lighter semi-professional camera than its pro sibling, and my very first digital camera. Now used only paired with the 5D. When capturing the moment matters more than the weight of two cameras.
Sony RX10 IV camera
1" CMOS sensor, 24–600mm optical zoom (f/2.4–4.0) My most-used camera. Light and versatile as an all-rounder compromise. It lets me go from wide-angle landscapes all the way to 600mm while keeping quality that has little to envy from a professional setup.
Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra
My lifeline against fleeting moments when I don't have a camera with me. Even with a quad camera featuring a 200 MP main sensor, a 50 MP ultra-wide, a 50 MP 5x telephoto, and a 10 MP 3x telephoto, like any smartphone it doesn't compete with real cameras, but it's enough so I don't regret leaving them behind.
Canon EF 24-70mm f/2.8L USM lens
Professional standard ultrasonic f/2.8 zoom The quintessential quality all-rounder between wide-angle and medium telephoto. Bright, solid, and immediate. The most used lens of all.
Canon EF 70-200mm f/2.8L IS USM
Professional telephoto zoom, constant f/2.8, image stabilized Heavy and you feel it. Brought along on planned missions! When I'm out on the hunt and distance matters. It compresses perspective and separates the subject with soft bokeh.
Canon EF 100mm f/2.8L Macro IS USM
Stabilized macro and medium telephoto lens, f/2.8 When I want to dive into tiny details, as well as for portraits and close-up views with a clean, precise rendering.
